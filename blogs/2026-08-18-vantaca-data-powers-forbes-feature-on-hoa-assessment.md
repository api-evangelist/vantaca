Vantaca Data Powers Forbes Feature on Special Assessments Forbes published a feature on August 13, 2026 examining the rising cost of HOA and condo assessments as an underreported line item in American homeownership. The piece, written by real estate contributor Jeffrey Steele , framed assessments alongside mortgages, insurance, property taxes, and maintenance as a growing financial pressure point for the more than 75 million Americans living in managed communities.
