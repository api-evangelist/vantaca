Yahoo! Finance published a feature story exploring why buyers are increasingly avoiding pre-2000 buildings due to escalating HOA fees and the rising risk of special assessments. Yahoo Finance's Senior Reporter, Claire Boston developed a story around a proprietary data analysis from the Vantaca platform that segmented condo fee trends by building age for the first time in national media coverage.
